# Architecture

Rendered figure: `results/week7/fig_architecture.png` (generated by `python -m curriculum_engine.run week7`).

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    subgraph inputs[Inputs]
        D[NMIMS semester syllabus booklets<br/>B TECH.zip, parsed locally]
        J[Job descriptions JD1-JD5]
    end
    subgraph g9[Group 9 - Curriculum Intelligence Engine]
        P[parse.py<br/>course records] --> F[data.py<br/>families, programmes, split]
        F --> E[embed.py<br/>MiniLM chunk embeddings]
        E --> C1[overlap / redundancy]
        E --> C2[prerequisites v2]
        E --> C3[diff / Bloom / emerging]
        E --> M[modernize.py<br/>skills, gaps, draft syllabus]
        C1 & C2 & C3 & M --> API[FastAPI api.py]
    end
    D --> P
    J --> M
    API --> UI[Committee UI<br/>Streamlit]
    M -- "POST /process-notice {doc_id, text}" --> G11[Group 11<br/>Communications Intelligence]
    M -- "passage table CSV" --> G4[Group 4<br/>Knowledge Assistant]
    G11 --> MAIL[emails to faculty / students]
    G4 --> QA[answers: prerequisites, labs, credits]
    UI --> BOS[Board of Studies decides]
```

The 31 October demo chain follows the instructor's plan: Group 9's draft syllabus goes to Group 11 (communication) and to
Group 4 (student questions). Group 10 (Administrative Copilot) can consume the same draft syllabus JSON for elective advice.
