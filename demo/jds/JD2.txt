Role: NLP Research Engineer, Domain Adaptation of LLMs (Legal and Healthcare)
Responsibilities:
- Adapt large language models to legal and clinical text through continued pre-training and supervised fine-tuning.
- Align models with human preferences using reinforcement learning from human feedback and direct preference optimisation.
- Curate, deduplicate and filter domain corpora, and design annotation guidelines for preference data.
- Evaluate models for factual accuracy, bias and safety before release.
Requirements:
- Solid foundations in probability, linear algebra and deep learning.
- Experience with transformer language models, tokenizers and the Hugging Face ecosystem.
- Familiarity with responsible AI practices and model documentation.
