SYNTHETIC SAMPLE JOB DESCRIPTION, written for the Group 9 demo (not a real posting)
Role: LLM Fine-Tuning Engineer, Customer Support Assistant
Responsibilities:
- Fine-tune open-weight large language models for multi-turn customer support conversations using supervised fine-tuning.
- Apply parameter-efficient fine-tuning methods such as LoRA and QLoRA with the Hugging Face Transformers and PEFT libraries.
- Build and clean instruction-tuning datasets from support tickets, removing personal data before training.
- Evaluate fine-tuned models with automatic metrics and human evaluation, and track hallucination rates.
Requirements:
- Strong Python and PyTorch skills, and experience training transformer models on GPUs.
- Understanding of tokenization, attention and the transformer architecture.
- Experience with prompt engineering and few-shot prompting as a baseline before fine-tuning.
